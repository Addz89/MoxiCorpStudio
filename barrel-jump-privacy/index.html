Quick privacy summary

Barrel Jump does not run advertising trackers or send your local high-score data to Moxi Corp. Your high-score name, scores and sound setting are stored locally on your Windows device. Optional Extra Life purchases are handled through Microsoft Store checkout.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Barrel Jump, a Windows PC game published by Moxi Corp. It describes the information handled by the current version of the game and the information you may choose to provide when contacting us.

Last updated: 13 August 2026.

2. Information stored locally on your device

Barrel Jump can save a player name or nickname for the local high-score table, the associated score, and your sound setting. This information is stored on your Windows device so that your scores and preference can remain available between game sessions.

  • High-score name or nickname you choose to enter.
  • Your local high scores.
  • Your sound on/off preference.

In the current Windows build, this data is stored in the local Barrel Jump application-data folder, normally under %LOCALAPPDATA%\BarrelJump.

Barrel Jump does not send these local high-score or settings files to Moxi Corp. Avoid entering sensitive information as your high-score name.

3. Microsoft Store purchases

Barrel Jump offers an optional Extra Life consumable purchase. The purchase flow is opened and processed through the Microsoft Store commerce system using the Microsoft account signed into the Store.

Moxi Corp does not receive or store your full payment-card details through Barrel Jump. The game receives the Store result needed to verify and fulfil the Extra Life purchase.

Microsoft independently processes information required to operate its Store, account and payment services under Microsoft's own terms and privacy practices.

Microsoft Privacy Statement

4. Information you provide when contacting support

If you contact Moxi Corp for support, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, your message, screenshots, device or error details, and any other information included in your support request.

We use that information to respond to you, troubleshoot issues, maintain the game, and handle relevant customer-service matters. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card details, or other information that is not needed to resolve your request.

5. Sharing, advertising and sale of personal information

Moxi Corp does not sell personal information collected through Barrel Jump. The current game does not include third-party advertising SDKs or behavioural advertising trackers.

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to operate support services, comply with law, protect rights or safety, or where you have requested or authorised the disclosure. Microsoft Store transactions are handled separately by Microsoft as described above.

6. Retention, access and deletion

Local game data

Local high scores and settings remain on your device until they are changed or removed. You can remove local Barrel Jump data by deleting the game's local application-data files or uninstalling/removing the relevant saved data from your device.

Support messages

Support communications may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond to the request, keep appropriate business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

You may contact us to ask about personal information that Moxi Corp holds about you through a support interaction, or to request correction or deletion where applicable.

7. Children and younger players

Barrel Jump does not intentionally ask players to submit age, date of birth, address or payment-card information directly to the game. Microsoft account, family-safety and Store purchase controls are managed through Microsoft services and device/account settings.

Parents and guardians should supervise purchases and encourage younger players to use a nickname rather than personal information in the local high-score name field.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the game and support processes secure. No method of electronic storage, device security or online communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so players should also keep Windows, Microsoft Store and account security settings up to date.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Barrel Jump changes, when we add new features or services, or when privacy requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
